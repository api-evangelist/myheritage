Germany has an exceptionally wide variety of family names (Familiennamen), with approximately one million distinct surnames. Some date back to the 12th century, and their origins can tell us about family histories, occupations, geographical features, physical and personality traits, and regional dialects. However, according to research by MyHeritage, this diversity is under threat.
